The photos illustrate a fictional household; they do not depict an actual flood or items actually lost by the presenter. Use them as BEFORE-loss photos in Hera.

The four receipts were created for this presentation. Northline Home and Riverbend Tech are fictional store identities in this scenario; the dates, SKUs, purchase amounts and transactions are invented. The receipts do not establish real ownership or purchases. Product dimensions and specifications on them are scenario details, not verified identities of the photographed items.

Replacement values in the verified catalogue are outputs from Hera's actual vision model during rehearsal. They are approximate CAD estimates, not verified retail prices or a promise of insurer reimbursement. The office and living room are separate stock-photo settings combined into one fictional loss scenario.
